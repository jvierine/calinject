# Manufacturer and service references

Consulted during Rev B/C development, 2026-11-23. Manufacturer PDFs remain at their original sources and are not redistributed. Prices are snapshots or estimates, not quotations. Rev C replacement specifications and stock references are in the [sourcing review](sourcing.md).

- [ADP-2-1+ datasheet](https://www.minicircuits.com/pdfs/ADP-2-1%2B.pdf), [JLC catalogue C2683460](https://jlcpcb.com/parts/2nd/RF_and_Wireless/RF_Power_Splitters__Combiners_2559), and [combiner isolation/matching](https://www.minicircuits.com/appdoc/AN10-006.html): Rev B transformer combiner and approximately $12.04 small-quantity allowance.
- [MAX-M10S datasheet](https://content.u-blox.com/sites/default/files/MAX-M10S_DataSheet_UBX-20035208.pdf) and [M10 SPG 5.10 interface](https://content.u-blox.com/sites/default/files/u-blox-M10-SPG-5.10_InterfaceDescription_UBX-21035062.pdf): pins, power, PPS and UBX configuration.
- [MAX-M10S distributor](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/u-blox/MAX-M10S-00B/15712906): $11.42 price reference.
- [KDS 1XTV10000MDA](https://atta.szlcsc.com/upload/public/pdf/source/20190315/C253701_69B1AE3584D7A6F1B7B612113CD52085.pdf): Rev C tunable oscillator, land pattern, keepout and tuning/noise limits. [ECS TXO32-S3](https://ecsxtal.com/store/pdf/ECS-TXO32-S3.pdf) applies to historical Rev B only.
- [TLV3501](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv3501.pdf), [SN74LVC1G14](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g14.pdf), [SN74LVC2G08](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2g08.pdf): comparator and pulse-generation logic.
- [SN74LVC2G125](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2g125.pdf): independently enabled, ballasted reference-output buffers.
- [Littelfuse 1206L](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/resettable-ptcs-1206l-datasheet?assetguid=2b6a1515-d4ee-4c83-8bd4-152b4901b8f5): PTC resistance, ratings and ordering suffixes.
- [MCP4725](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/APID/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/MCP4725-12-Bit-Digital-to-Analog-Converter-with-EEPROM-Memory-DS20002039.pdf): DAC pins, commands and address.
- [PE4312](https://www.psemi.com/pdf/datasheets/pe4312ds.pdf) and [distributor](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/psemi/PE4312C-Z/5878673): attenuation, serial interface, package and $4.40 reference.
- [Waveshare RP2040-Zero](https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/RP2040-Zero), [module schematic](https://files.waveshare.com/upload/4/4c/RP2040_Zero.pdf) and [MicroPython firmware](https://micropython.org/download/WAVESHARE_RP2040_ZERO/).
- [Bourns 3362](https://www.bourns.com/docs/Product-Datasheets/3362.pdf): trimmer dimensions.
- [BAT WIRELESS SMA drawing](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/ca832414c3d29ba9bc255fc7a4361c9c.pdf?productCode=C496549): BWSMA-KE-Z001, female center contact, 5.08 mm ground-pin spacing. Its $1.20 allowance is not a verified quote.
- [PCBWay assembly](https://www.pcbway.com/pcb-assembly.html) and [capabilities](https://www.pcbway.com/assembly-capabilities.html): proposed full-turnkey supplier. Acceptance of this BOM, programming and test scope still requires their review.
